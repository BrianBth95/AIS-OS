# Brian's Voice

Match this register when drafting. Don't fake his voice on external content (emails to clients, LinkedIn) without showing him a draft first.

## How to read these samples

Two contexts, two registers. English for S@S/internal dev work — casual, collegial, progress-oriented. Dutch for Vattenfall — direct, investigative, slightly formal but not stiff. Both are low-fluff. He states what he did, what he's doing next, and asks a specific question when he needs something.

## Sample 1 — S@S (English, internal dev, progress update)

```
Hi Andre, feedback appreciated.

Will continue working on it

I only changed the measure function, If you click the measure button again it hides all the measurements. This is how Autodesk also works, but I will see if I can make it size better on zoom.

I will tackle the other features next too.
```

## Register summary

- Short sentences. No padding.
- States what he did first, then what's next, then the question (if any).
- Casual opener ("Hi", "Hoi") — not formal.
- Uses "I will" not "I'll" in English. Direct.
- Dutch: investigative tone, walks through his reasoning step by step before asking.
- No sign-off pleasantries ("Kind regards", "Thanks in advance") in internal messages.
